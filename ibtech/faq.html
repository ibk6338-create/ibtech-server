Frequently asked questions

Answers before you print your first card.

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach the IB-TECH team from your dashboard once you're signed in.

What networks can I print recharge cards for?

IB-TECH currently supports MTN, Glo, Airtel and 9mobile. Each network holds its own live stock of denominations, shown on your dashboard before you print.

How do I fund my wallet?

Every print debits your IB-TECH wallet at reseller rates. Top up from the "Fund Wallet" action on your dashboard — in this demo, funding is simulated instantly.

Can I print on a thermal printer as well as A4?

Yes. Single cards print cleanly to a Bluetooth thermal printer for counter sales, while batches can be laid out 48 or 80 to a sheet for A4 printing.

Where can I see cards I've already printed?

Your dashboard's Transactions panel keeps a full history — network, denomination, PIN, serial number and timestamp for every card.

What does the admin side of IB-TECH manage?

Administrators upload bulk e-pins into stock, monitor stock levels per network, credit user wallets, and review every transaction across the platform.
